Everything a student needs to stay on track

One clean workspace for assignments, exams, the calendar and your progress.

Assignment tracking

Add, edit and complete assignments with subject, due date, importance and estimated effort. Search, filter and sort instantly.

Automatic priority list

Our unique engine scores every task and exam and keeps a ranked, colour-coded to-do list updated daily.

Smart calendar

See every deadline and exam on a month view, colour-coded by priority. Click a day to see details or add work.

Exam schedule

Keep dates, times, venues and syllabus together with live countdowns and revision tips as the exam gets closer.

Progress analytics

Completion rate, on-time rate, day streak, weekly output and per-subject progress – all in a single discipline score.

Deadline reminders

In-app alerts and optional browser notifications warn you about overdue work, tasks due soon and upcoming exams.

How the priority engine decides

No manual sorting. Three signals combine into one score for every task.

1

Urgency

Rises exponentially as the deadline approaches. Overdue work jumps straight to the top.

2

Importance

You mark each task Low, Medium or High. Exams are always treated as High.

3

Effort

Bigger tasks need an earlier start, so estimated hours add weight when the deadline is within a week.

score = urgency + importance + effort
